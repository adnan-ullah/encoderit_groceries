# Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Encoder Groceries (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application (the “App”).

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you use the App, we may collect the following information that you provide:

- **Account Information**: Name, email address, and password when you create an account.
- **Profile Details**: Optional information like profile photo, delivery address, and contact details.
- **Order Information**: Products added to cart, wishlist items, and order history.
- **Support Requests**: Information you provide when you contact us for help or feedback.

### 1.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you use the App, we may automatically collect:

- **Usage Data**: Screens viewed, features used, and basic interaction events within the App.
- **Device Information**: Device model, operating system version, app version, and anonymous identifiers.
- **Diagnostic Data**: Crash logs and performance data to improve stability and user experience.

We do **not** collect precise GPS location or sensitive personal information (such as payment card numbers or government-issued IDs) directly in this App. If payment services are integrated, they are handled by third-party providers under their own privacy policies.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

- **Provide and Maintain the Service**
  - Create and manage your account.
  - Enable browsing, searching, and purchasing of grocery products.
  - Manage your cart, wishlist, and order history.

- **Improve the App**
  - Analyze usage to improve features, performance, and usability.
  - Fix bugs and enhance security and stability.

- **Communicate With You**
  - Send transactional messages (e.g., order confirmations, updates).
  - Respond to your requests, questions, or feedback.

We do **not** sell your personal information to third parties.

## 3. Data Storage and Security

- Your account data may be stored securely on your device and/or on our servers or trusted third‑party infrastructure.
- We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.
- Despite our efforts, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 4. Third‑Party Services

The App may use third‑party services such as:

- **Analytics and Crash Reporting Providers**

These services may collect information as described in their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third‑party services used with the App.

- **Account Information**: You can update your account information (such as name or email) within the App where supported.
- **Access and Deletion**: You may request access to or deletion of your personal information by contacting us (see “Contact Us” below). We may retain certain information as required by law or for legitimate business purposes.
